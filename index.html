About & research interests

01 / APPROACH

From structural mechanics to data-efficient design.

I am an aerospace engineering PhD candidate at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid and a doctoral researcher in the Aeroelastic and Structural Design (ASD) Lab. My work focuses on making the design of composite airframes more efficient through automated finite-element modeling, structural optimization, and machine learning.

I develop computational workflows that connect aircraft geometry and internal structural layouts to analysis-ready models, sizing studies, and surrogate-assisted optimization. My current research tackles mixed-variable design spaces where each evaluation is expensive and strength and buckling constraints make feasible designs difficult to find.

My background is in Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics at the University of Patras, where I graduated first in my department in 2024. My diploma research on tow-steered wings established my interest in using composite tailoring and numerical optimization to improve structural and aeroelastic performance.

Research interests

  • Composite structures and aeroelastic tailoring
  • Coupled structural layout and sizing optimization
  • Gaussian processes and constrained Bayesian optimization
  • Automated finite-element modeling and analysis

Directions I want to explore

Stacking-sequence optimization, gradient-enhanced and multi-fidelity methods, physics-informed learning, and closer integration of aeroelastic loads and constraints.

02 / SELECTED WORK
Current research · In progress

Scalable Bayesian optimization
for composite wingboxes

How can we explore a large aircraft design space when every structural analysis is expensive?

I am developing a constrained, multi-center trust-region framework that searches layout and sizing variables together. Sparse Gaussian processes model structural responses, while a physics-informed mass residual model combines an analytical estimate with learned corrections.

Constrained BOSparse Gaussian processesMixed-variable design
Methodology & current scope

The ongoing study compares global, single-center and multi-center strategies under strength and buckling constraints. Shared surrogate models support several local search regions, with layout diversity used to explore distinct configurations. The manuscript and accompanying technical notes are in development; results are not presented here as peer-reviewed findings.

Submitted manuscript

Nested layout & sizing optimization

A surrogate-assisted outer loop explores structural layouts, while an inner gradient-based solver sizes each configuration. Automated Python and HyperMesh TCL modeling connects design choices to finite-element analysis.

About the submitted work

Nested Surrogate-Based Layout and Size Optimization of Composite Wingbox Structures, N. Ziakos and A. Cini. Submitted to Elsevier / Composite Structures. The study investigates noise-aware Bayesian optimization and a staged multi-run sizing procedure. Publication details will be added after an editorial decision.

Diploma thesis · 2024

Aeroelastic tailoring via tow-steering

Investigating how curved fiber paths can improve the mass and aeroelastic performance of high-aspect-ratio wings. An FE/DLM model of the NASA uCRM-13.5 wing couples structural analysis with MATLAB-based optimization.
