About & research interests

01 / APPROACH

From structural mechanics to data-efficient design.

I am a PhD candidate in Aerospace Engineering at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid and a doctoral researcher in the Aeroelastic and Structural Design (ASD) Lab. My research focuses on improving the early-stage design of composite airframes by combining structural mechanics, automated parametric Finite Element (FE) modeling, and Machine Learning.

I develop computational workflows that connect aircraft geometry and internal structural layouts to FE analysis and design optimization, from single wingboxes up to global FE models of a complete airframe. My work combines gradient-based methods with Gaussian Process (GP) surrogate models and Bayesian optimization to explore high-dimensional, mixed-integer design spaces efficiently. My goal is to enable large-scale, multidisciplinary optimization of the whole airframe, involving layout and composite optimization, through the combination of gradient-based methods and surrogate-assisted Bayesian optimization.

My background is in Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics at the University of Patras, where I graduated first in my department in 2024. My diploma research on tow-steered wings established my interest in using composite tailoring and numerical optimization to improve structural and aeroelastic performance.

02 / SELECTED WORK
Current research · Airframe modeling

Parametric global FE models
of a complete airframe

Can a full-aircraft structural model be rebuilt automatically whenever the design changes, early enough to inform the layout?

I am developing a Python framework that generates a global finite-element model of a half airframe of a single-aisle reference transport aircraft — fuselage, wing, pylon, horizontal stabilizer and fin — directly from its CPACS geometry definition. Planners first lay out every frame, stringer, rib, spar, floor, door and cutout; HyperMesh then builds and meshes the structure, and the assembled model is loaded and analysed in OptiStruct.

Global FE modelsHyperMesh Python APIOptiStructAVL / VSPAEROOpen CASCADE
Pipeline & current scope

The model has around 300,000 nodes and 335,000 shell elements, with beam stiffeners, joints between the parts, and a mass model of systems, payload and fuel. Aerodynamic loads for trimmed symmetric manoeuvres come from a vortex-lattice (AVL) or panel-method (OpenVSP/VSPAERO) model, and the analysis covers free vibration, static strength, linear buckling and cabin pressure. Geometry can be built natively in HyperMesh or exported as STEP sheets from Open CASCADE. A complete rebuild and analysis runs unattended in about two hours.

The structure has received an initial fully stressed sizing; coupling it with formal optimization and static aeroelastic load relief is the next step. The framework builds on the parametric approach presented at EASN 2025, AIAA SciTech 2026 and ICAS 2026. The code is in a private GitHub repository.

Current research · In progress

High-dimensional Bayesian optimization
of wing layout and laminates

How can we optimize structural layout and composite laminates together when every evaluation needs a new FE mesh?

Changing the number of ribs or stringers changes the mesh, so no gradients exist with respect to these variables. I am developing a multi-trust-region Bayesian optimization framework that treats layout and laminate variables jointly, with sparse multi-output Gaussian-process models of the strength and buckling constraints.

Multi-trust-region BOMulti-output sparse GPsLamination parametersMixed-integer design
Methodology & current scope

The composite wingbox is described by regional layout variables (rib and separate upper and lower stringer counts), metallic rib thicknesses and either 72 smeared ply-thickness variables or 144 variables that add bending lamination parameters to represent the stacking sequence. Element strains, rib stresses and buckling factors are aggregated regionally and enforced as strict constraints. Several trust regions with layout-diverse centres share the surrogate models and contribute candidates to each parallel batch, and stacking sequences are retrieved to satisfy manufacturing ply-book rules.

Preliminary campaigns find feasible designs much lighter than the best initial sample with both laminate descriptions. A comparison of the two under the same manufacturing rules, and against a global mixed-integer solver, is in progress for AIAA AVIATION 2027.

AIAA SciTech 2027 · Accepted

Multi-trust-region BO for coupled layout & sizing

Joint optimization of rib count, stringer count, spar-cap width and 75 ply-count and rib-thickness variables, with every candidate evaluated by a regenerated FE model. Sparse variational GPs, including multi-output models and a residual correction to a geometry-based mass estimate, drive constrained Thompson sampling or a smoothed merit expected improvement in single or multiple concurrent trust regions.

Multi-Trust-Region Bayesian Optimization for Coupled Layout and Sizing of a Composite Wingbox, N. Ziakos and A. Cini. Multi-region variants reached lighter designs than their single-region counterparts and a global acquisition, and the best multi-region variant reached a lower mass than a global mixed-integer solver with the same evaluation budget. Composite Structures · 2027 · Open access

Nested layout & sizing optimization

A Gaussian-process outer loop explores the wingbox layout (rib count, stringer count and spar-cap width), while OptiStruct gradient-based sizing optimizes each configuration. A staged multi-run sizing strategy reduced structural mass by up to 450 kg relative to the first feasible sizing result and reduced run-to-run variability.

Gaussian processesOptiStructPython / TCL
About the published work

Nested surrogate-based layout and size optimization of composite wingbox structures, N. Ziakos and A. Cini, Composite Structures 402 (2027) 121103. Three Bayesian optimization repetitions for each of three initial datasets, with 30 layout evaluations per study, all reduced the initial best mass; selected layouts typically had 50 mm spar caps and 13–14 stringers. Results were benchmarked against a global gradient-free optimizer.
